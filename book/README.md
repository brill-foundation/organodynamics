## The two editions

The book is published in two editions, and the split is itself an act of the discipline it
describes — the discipline keeps its history append-only and never renegotiates what it used
to believe (RFC-001 §7). So the earlier understanding is sealed and honoured, and the later
one grows beside it.

### 🔒 [Recovery Edition](recovery-edition/) — *sealed, Stage 1*
The most faithful reconstruction of the book as it existed at the first repository snapshot:
RFC-001, RFC-002, and the chapter skeleton, woven into nine chapters (The Door → Open
Questions). **Frozen for historical fidelity** — not revised, not modernised, not continued.
A research notebook in pencil. → [SEAL](recovery-edition/SEAL.md) ·
[Table of Contents](recovery-edition/SUMMARY.md)

### 🌱 [Living Edition](living-edition/) — *active, Stage 2*
Continues the book from everything after: the Development Manifest and its constitutional
review, the Substrate, Meta Review, Recognition, the Discovery Engine, the Seasons, and the
laboratory (Ch. 9–14). It carries **three narrative layers** (Technical · Human · Chronicle),
a full **visual epistemology** (pencil → ink → colour), and a **maturation gradient** — the
book matures from notebook to specification as you read it. → [Start here](living-edition/README.md) ·
[Table of Contents](living-edition/SUMMARY.md)

## How to trust it

The book is a **derived artifact** — a lens; the repository is the sky. Every doctrinal
paragraph cites its source (an RFC section, a research document, a journal event) and can be
re-derived from the source of truth in [`/rfcs`](../rfcs), [`/research`](../research),
[`/reviews`](../reviews), and [`/journal`](../journal). If the book and the Record ever
disagree, the Record wins. See the Living Edition's
[Reading the Record](living-edition/apparatus/reading-the-record.md).
