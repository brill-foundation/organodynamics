# Glossary

*The canonical vocabulary of Organodynamics, as used throughout the manuscript. Each entry
gives the settled sense and its source. Terms are cross-linked to the chapters where they live.
Where the discipline itself has not settled a term, the entry says so.*

---

### Aggregation (prohibited)
Combining several Instruments' readings into a single scalar. **Forbidden.** The Observatory
*juxtaposes* readings and never averages them, "because disagreement is information" — averaging
destroys exactly the signal the Observatory exists to produce. A composite score is "a single
Instrument reborn, its theory of what matters hidden inside the weights."
*Source: R1 §6. See [Chapter 6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### Ambient observation
The "sky survey" mode: standing Instruments taking readings at a steady cadence. The discipline
defined this mode first. Contrast **directed observation**.
*Source: R2 §1. See [Chapter 3](../manuscript/03-come.md).*

### Challenge
The discipline's method of settling things — a discourse in which anyone may push on a
representation. Merit is adjudicated by challenge, not measured. Every representation, RFC, and
this book is "resolved by challenge and revision." A preserved rejection is "the first negative
result."
*Source: R1 §5.3, §10. See [Chapter 2](../manuscript/02-organodynamics.md).*

### Confidence
A representation's declared degree of certainty, stated by its author (e.g. "speculative"). A
first-class part of a door. Must **never** be aggregated per author into a competence score — the
moment it is, "honest uncertainty dies."
*Source: R1 §4, §5.3. See [Chapter 5](../manuscript/05-personal-worlds.md).*

### Constitutive event
An event *internal* to the discipline — a commit, a challenge, a supersession — for which "the
recording is the act": the Record cannot be wrong about it. One of the Record's **two zones**.
Contrast **testimony event**.
*Source: R1 §7. See [Chapter 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md).*

### Declared observables
What an author *asserts* about a representation: status, confidence, registered tensions. One of
the two sensing channels. Contrast **derived observables**; the richest reading is the **gap**
between them.
*Source: R1 §5.2. See [Chapter 5](../manuscript/05-personal-worlds.md).*

### Derived observables
What the Record *implies* regardless of assertion: event frequency, dormancy, lineage depth, age
of confidence since last challenge.
*Source: R1 §5.2. See [Chapter 5](../manuscript/05-personal-worlds.md).*

### Directed observation
The "targeted campaign" mode: Instruments composed around a *live question*, then dispersed. Its
recorded form is an **Inquiry**. Contrast **ambient observation**.
*Source: R2 §1. See [Chapter 3](../manuscript/03-come.md).*

### Dissolved (tension state)
A tension that was "apparent, not real" — two doors that looked incompatible and weren't. Not an
error to delete but **data**: it teaches what looked incompatible and wasn't.
*Source: R1 §5.5. See [Chapter 6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### Door
The discipline's working name for a **representation**. You *open* a door; you do not publish it.
The metaphor is exact: a door is open by nature, walked through, and can lead somewhere you did
not expect. The hinge on which the whole book's chapter interlock turns.
*Source: R1 §5.4, §5.3. See [Chapter 0](../manuscript/00-the-door.md).*

### Dormancy
How long a representation has gone untouched. A **reading**, never a deficiency: "a door that
sits for a year is an observation, not a deficiency." The dormancy report is "a lamp, not an
alarm."
*Source: R1 §5.3. See [Chapter 0](../manuscript/00-the-door.md).*

### The gap
The distance between what an author *declares* about a door and how the door *behaves* in the
Record. "The richest signal" the discipline can read — e.g. a door declared load-bearing but
behaviorally dormant and long unchallenged. Points at the claim, never the person.
*Source: R1 §5.2. See [Chapters 5](../manuscript/05-personal-worlds.md),
[6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### The Goodhart line
The boundary principle of measurement: "do not measure anything that, once measured, would
redirect authors toward the measure and away from reality." Not the deepest reason for the
discipline's restraint — that is "disagreement is information" — but "what punishes the
discipline that forgets" it.
*Source: R1 §5.3, §6. See [Chapters 5](../manuscript/05-personal-worlds.md),
[6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### Held (tension state)
An **accepted paradox**: two doors that resist each other and neither gives way. Quietly asserts
that "reality, as currently representable, sustains both poles." The discipline's "generative
organ" — the most common birthplace of a new representation.
*Source: R1 §5.5. See [Chapter 6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### Inquiry
An Observatory composed *dynamically* around a live question. Anatomy: a **question**, a
**composed roster** of Instruments, a **termination condition**, and a **disposition**. Cheap,
plural, recorded, contestable. Not a hierarchy over the Observatory.
*Source: R2 §2–§4. See [Chapter 3](../manuscript/03-come.md).*

### Instrument
"A versioned, reproducible function from a record to observations, together with the protocol
that makes its readings comparable across time and observers." Second-order (observes
representations, never reality); constituted by protocol, not machinery; bound by three
commitments — minimally disturbing, reproducible, versioned.
*Source: R1 §3. See [Chapter 2](../manuscript/02-organodynamics.md).*

### Observation
A reading: `Instrument(Record, t)`. **Unauthored, confidence-free, reproducible.** Immutable "for
free" — re-derivable from its coordinates `(at-commit, instrument-version)`. Never corrected; the
Instrument is recalibrated instead.
*Source: R1 §4, §5.6. See [Chapter 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md).*

### The Observatory
"A set of Instruments over a single shared Record, together with a comparison protocol." Kept
**flat, not towered** — every comparator is itself just another function of the Record. Its two
disciplines: **no aggregation**, and **commissioning by challenge**.
*Source: R1 §6. See [Chapter 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md).*

### Promotion
The act by which an *interpreted* observation becomes a representation — it "enters the front
door like any other representation, as a door," with its own confidence and open to challenge.
How the discipline studies itself without a special machinery for doing so.
*Source: R1 §5.4. See [Chapter 0](../manuscript/00-the-door.md).*

### Reality
The anchor: "not negotiated, not produced, not revisable." Not an object *of* the system but
what the system is *about* — "the only element of the diagram that nothing else produces."
Above the Record (a correction made in revision 3).
*Source: R1 §7. See [Chapter 7](../manuscript/07-the-sun.md).*

### Recalibration
The correct response to a faulty reading: never edit the reading — fix the Instrument's function,
bump its version, take a new reading. Both readings remain derivable forever. *Who* may
recalibrate is an open question (R1 Q-b).
*Source: R1 §5.6. See [Chapters 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md),
[8](../manuscript/08-open-questions.md).*

### The Record
"Append-only history of everything that happened. Evidence, not knowledge." An **artifact**, not
the anchor — infallible only about itself (constitutive events), and about Reality "exactly as
good as its witnesses" (testimony events). Its append-only rule is "an engineered homage" to
Reality's incorruptibility.
*Source: R1 §4, §7. See [Chapter 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md).*

### Relation
A **second-order** object *about representations* — tension, supersession, dependency. "Exists
only between its endpoints" and has no meaning alone. Whether relations beyond tension and
supersession are needed is open (R1 Q-d).
*Source: R1 §4, §5.5. See [Chapter 6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### Representation
"Authored claims about reality." Bear confidence, mature through a lifecycle, can be challenged.
The discipline's **first-order** object; its working name is **door**.
*Source: R1 §4. See [Chapter 0](../manuscript/00-the-door.md).*

### Restraint
What an Instrument refuses to measure — "defined as much by its restraint as by its reach." The
four prohibitions (people, merit, velocity-as-virtue, truth) are restraint stated as law.
*Source: R1 §5.1, §5.3. See [Chapter 5](../manuscript/05-personal-worlds.md).*

### STATE (cached reading)
Any `STATE` file in the repository is "a cache of the latest reading, never a document." It must
carry its coordinates (`taken-at`, `at-commit`, `instrument-version`), must never be hand-edited,
and must never be cited by a representation.
*Source: R1 §5.7. See [Chapter 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md).*

### Tension
A **relation** in which two doors pull against each other. Claim-like (a declared tension can be
wrong) with its own lifecycle: *open → active → resolved / held / dissolved.* "The generative
organ of the discipline."
*Source: R1 §5.5. See [Chapter 6](../manuscript/06-hospitality.md).*

### Testimony event
An event *external* to the discipline — an experiment, a conversation, an encounter with Reality
— which "enters the Record only as testimony": the Record proves it was *given*, never that it
was *true*. One of the Record's **two zones**. Contrast **constitutive event**.
*Source: R1 §7. See [Chapter 4](../manuscript/04-reality-layers.md).*
