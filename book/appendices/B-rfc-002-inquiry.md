# RFC-002 — Inquiry

**Epistemic status:** a question, barely a proposal. RFC-001 exposed it; this RFC
exists so it is not lost. Deliberately thin.

## 1. The exposure

The Observatory (RFC-001 §6) observes — but observation has two modes, and RFC-001
defines only one of them.

- **Ambient observation**: standing Instruments taking readings at cadence. The sky
  survey. RFC-001's subject.
- **Directed observation**: Instruments composed around a live question. The
  targeted campaign. Undefined.

The undefined mode is forced by ordinary events: a new tension appears; a
representation changes; a reality layer accumulates evidence. Which Instruments
participate in the inquiry that follows — and who decides?

## 2. The proposal-shaped intuition

An **Inquiry** composes an Observatory dynamically around a question, rather than
assuming a fixed observational pipeline. A sketch of its anatomy:

- **a question** — what the Inquiry exists to understand;
- **a composed roster** — which Instruments are brought to bear, chosen for this
  question, not permanently;
- **a termination condition** — what ends it: answer, dissolution, or abandonment;
- **a disposition** — what becomes of its findings: promotion into a representation
  (RFC-001 §5.4), dissolution of the triggering tension, or nothing — recorded
  either way.

Inquiries are recorded objects: opening one is an event in the Record. Competing
Inquiries into the same question are legal and desirable.

## 3. The known danger

Composing a roster is measurement design. The choice of Instruments embodies a
theory of what matters — the exact problem RFC-001 §2 names, reappearing one level
up. There is no vantage-free composition.

The defense therefore cannot be composing *correctly*. It must be (proposed):
Inquiries are **cheap, plural, recorded, and contestable** — the composition is
never invisible and never exclusive. Anyone may open a competing Inquiry with a
different roster into the same question; the disagreement between their findings is
information (RFC-001 §6).

Inquiry also buys something underappreciated: it **localizes disturbance in time**.
A campaign disturbs the specimen for a season; a standing pipeline is a panopticon.

## 4. Not a hierarchy

Inquiry must not become an authority layer above the Observatory. It is a dynamic
process — a way of convening Instruments — not a command structure over them.
Whether "who may open an Inquiry" needs any restriction at all is open; the default
position is that it does not.

## 5. Open questions

- **Q-a.** Can an Inquiry be opened by an event (a tension appearing) as well as by
  a researcher? If Inquiries open automatically, is automatic observation a
  disturbance the Goodhart line forbids?
- **Q-b.** What terminates an Inquiry — and is abandonment itself a recorded
  finding? (An Inquiry nobody finished may be the dormancy signal of the question
  layer.)
- **Q-c.** Do competing Inquiries into one question need a comparison protocol of
  their own — the Observatory problem, one level up — and does the tower stop
  there?
- **Q-d.** Is "disagreement is information" the natural termination criterion: an
  Inquiry ends when the disagreement it was opened to understand has been
  metabolized, not suppressed?

## 6. Disposition

Resolved by challenge and revision like any other RFC. If the ambient/directed
distinction proves unreal, this RFC should be rejected and absorbed into RFC-001 —
and the rejection kept, as always.
