# The Two Editions

*The Brill Blueprint exists as two editions, and the split is itself an act of the discipline
the book describes.*

The discipline keeps its history append-only and never renegotiates what happened (RFC-001
§7). Applied to the book itself, that principle produces not one manuscript but two: an
earlier understanding, sealed and honoured, and a later one growing beside it.

| | **Recovery Edition** | **Living Edition** |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage** | 1 — sealed | 2 — active |
| **Purpose** | historical fidelity | continuation |
| **Covers** | the first snapshot: RFC-001, RFC-002, the chapter skeleton | everything after: the Manifest, Substrate, Meta Review, Recognition, the Discovery Engine, the Seasons, the laboratory |
| **Register** | a research notebook (pencil) | notebook → engineering document → specification |
| **Voices** | one (recovered prose) | three (Technical · Human · Chronicle) |
| **Status** | **frozen** — not revised, not continued | **living** — absorbs new canon, never erasing old |
| **Where** | [`book/recovery-edition/`](../../recovery-edition/) · [SEAL](../../recovery-edition/SEAL.md) | you are here |

## Why not just revise the first edition?

Because revising it would *erase* it — and the discipline forbids erasing what it used to
believe. The Recovery Edition is the book's first **held state**: the pencil sketch from
which the ink was later drawn. Read together, the two editions let you watch the discipline
mature not only in its ideas but in *the book itself* — which is the whole point.

- The **Recovery Edition** is where the discipline was *discovered* — warm, philosophical,
  exploratory. Its chapters (The Door, Vision, Reality Layers, The Sun…) are the seeds.
- The **Living Edition** is where the discipline *turned on itself* — governing its own
  development, building a substrate, reducing itself to a discovery engine, and finally
  building a laboratory to run itself. Its chapters (The Manifest, The Substrate, The
  Discovery Engine, The Open Constitution…) are the growth.

## Reading paths

- **Straight through the whole arc:** Recovery Edition Ch. 0–8, then Living Edition Ch. 9–14.
  You will feel the register mature from notebook to specification — the Chrysalis performed.
- **Just today's understanding:** start here, [How to Read the Living Edition](01-how-to-read.md),
  then the [Table of Contents](../SUMMARY.md).
- **Just what happened:** read the [Chronicle](../chronicle/00-chronicle.md) end to end — the
  project's true history in five acts.
- **Audit anything:** every doctrinal claim carries provenance to an RFC or a journal event;
  the sources live in [`/rfcs`](../../../rfcs) and [`/journal`](../../../journal) and can be
  re-derived. The book is a lens; the repository is the sky.
