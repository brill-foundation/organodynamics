# Glossary — Living Edition additions

*New canonical vocabulary introduced after the first snapshot. Extends the
[Recovery Edition Glossary](../../recovery-edition/apparatus/glossary.md), which still holds
for door, Instrument, Observatory, Reality, tension, and the rest. Each entry gives the
settled sense, its medium (how mature), and its source.*

---

### Attention 🔵
The scarce constitutional resource. The journal is unbounded; a person is not. Something
decides which commitments meet reality and which wait — and that decision is where bias and
capture enter. Its allocation must be plural and visible. *Source: RFC-004 §5; THEORY-001
Chain 7. → [Ch. 12](../manuscript/12-the-discovery-engine.md).*

### Authority (as a lease) 🔵
The right to perform a constitutive act (ratify, commission, merge into canon). Its correct
form is a **lease** — scoped to acts, granted by a recorded event, time-limited, revocable,
never derived from identity. Every exercise must cite the grant event it acts under, or it is
void. *Source: RFC-004 §9. → [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md).*

### Closure 🔵
The engine's mechanisms are themselves commitments — exposed, consequence-bearing,
remembered. *Governance is the loop run on itself, not a layer above it.* Every knowledge
institution that decayed, decayed here: rules exempted from the rules. *Source: THEORY-001
Chain 6. → [Ch. 12](../manuscript/12-the-discovery-engine.md).*

### Commitment ⚫
The discipline's ontological primitive: a claim staked, by someone, at a time, in a form that
cannot be quietly revised after the encounter. What makes an event *epistemic* rather than
archival. *Source: RFC-004 §3; THEORY-001 §2. → [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md).*

### The CV rule 🔵
A Personal Research Profile is a claim *authored by its subject*, every item citing journal
events; the system verifies the citations and does nothing else. A system-computed profile is
a forbidden observation-about-a-person; a self-authored one is a challengeable claim. *"Keep
the citation; kill the counter."* *Source: RFC-005 §3.1. → [Ch. 11](../manuscript/11-meta-review-and-recognition.md).*

### The discovery engine ✏️🔵
The loop — **commit, expose, bind consequence, remember** — run under verifiable plurality,
closed over its own rules, throttled by visibly-allocated attention, against an outside that
answers without negotiating. Six elements. *Locked* until X2. *Source: THEORY-001 §3.
→ [Ch. 12](../manuscript/12-the-discovery-engine.md).*

### The doorway / D-0 🔴
The doorway is the recorded path (`./ods note`) made the path of least resistance. **D-0** is
the first-stranger experiment: will any non-Claude process write to the record? Registered
prediction: *demand*, not friction, is the likelier failure. *Source: SEASON-1-DOORWAY-PLAN.
→ [Ch. 13](../manuscript/13-the-laboratory-and-the-seasons.md).*

### Event ⚫
The substrate primitive: *at a moment in time, a provenanced process committed some content,
in relation to prior commitments* — `(time, process, content, references)`. Deliberately
almost meaningless; meaning is assigned above the waist by lenses. *Source: RFC-004 §2.
→ [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md).*

### Exposure 🔵
The arranged collision of a commitment with something its author does not control — reality
(experiment) or other minds (challenge). A system of commitments never exposed is an archive
of opinions. *Source: THEORY-001 Chain 2. → [Ch. 12](../manuscript/12-the-discovery-engine.md).*

### Lens 🔵
A versioned, declared typing that interprets meaning-free events as claims, challenges,
questions. **Lenses are themselves representations** — challengeable, competing, retirable —
so the ontology never has to be settled by decree. *Source: RFC-004 §4.
→ [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md).*

### Meta Review 🔵
A directed Inquiry over a corpus of independent reviews, producing *maps* (reproducible) kept
apart from a *recommendation* (authored). Makes integration visible; quorum = three
independent processes; never a verdict, never aggregation. *Source: RFC-003.
→ [Ch. 11](../manuscript/11-meta-review-and-recognition.md).*

### The narrow waist 🔵
The design pattern of the substrate: make the fixed part tiny and theory-poor, let everything
opinionated churn above it. Fixes exactly what the constitution must fix (that something
happened, when, by whom, in relation to what) and nothing else. *Source: RFC-004 §1, §6.
→ [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md).*

### Ratification / Q-f 🔴
The rule by which anything becomes canon — *the project's largest open wound*. It does not
exist. The operative substitute is **ratification-by-continuation** (the next message builds
on it), which means the founder decides by default. *Source: RFC-003 Q-f; READING-001 §4.1.
→ [Ch. 9](../manuscript/09-the-manifest.md), [Ch. 14](../manuscript/14-the-open-constitution.md).*

### The reflexivity trap 🔴
Meta-work is always safer than work: one level up nothing can be wrong yet, and the
discipline's vocabulary makes ascent feel like progress. A system built to metabolise
disagreement can spend its life metabolising itself. *Active now.* *Source: RFC-004 §11;
THEORY-001 §8.7. → [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md), [Ch. 12](../manuscript/12-the-discovery-engine.md).*

### Season 🔵
A unit of development reframed as a directed Inquiry over the project's own next step: a
question, a roster, a termination condition, a disposition. A cancelled season is a rejected
RFC — kept, with reasons. *Source: SEASONS. → [Ch. 13](../manuscript/13-the-laboratory-and-the-seasons.md).*

### The seven invariants ✏️
The proposed entrenched core: Supremacy of Reality; the Record; Plurality; Persons-are-not-
specimens; Provenance; Ratification & Stewardship; Exit. Proposed, **not ratified** (no rule
exists to ratify them). *Source: RFC-000 review §7; RFC-004 §7.
→ [Ch. 9](../manuscript/09-the-manifest.md), [Ch. 14](../manuscript/14-the-open-constitution.md).*

### View 🔵
A lens applied to the journal at a coordinate, rendered for a bounded quantum of attention. A
document is a view — derived, re-derivable, disposable, *never authoritative*. Generalises
RFC-001 §5.7 to every document. *Source: RFC-004 §5. → [Ch. 10](../manuscript/10-the-substrate.md).*

### X2 (the lock) 🔴
The experiment that must run before THEORY-001 may be built upon: an independent, non-Claude
process reads the same record without seeing READING-001/THEORY-001, and reproduces or
diverges from them. Until it returns, the theory is one instrument's artifact until proven
otherwise. *Source: THEORY-001 §9, §11. → [Ch. 12](../manuscript/12-the-discovery-engine.md).*
