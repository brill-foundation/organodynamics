# Gaps & Horizon

*What the book does not yet have, and how it stays alive. In this discipline an open question
is a first-class object, not a failure — so this register is written the way the discipline
writes its own: each gap named, sourced, and tracked to what would close it.*

The Recovery Edition's [Outstanding Gaps](../../recovery-edition/editorial/07-outstanding-gaps.md)
still stand for that edition. This register covers the Living Edition and the project's own
open frontier.

---

## Part 1 — Gaps in the book

### LG-1 · The book cannot reach black ink, and should not — yet 🔴
- **State:** the specification-register chapters (12–14) reach ⚫ black ink only rarely.
- **Why:** the discipline has **ratified nothing** (Q-f is open). Black ink means canonical;
  almost nothing qualifies.
- **Closes when:** the ratification rule is established and something is ratified under it —
  then blue passages become black and the Chronicle Log records the day.
- **This is honesty, not incompleteness.** The medium is telling the truth.

### LG-2 · The chapters cross-link the RFCs rather than re-copying them 🔵
- **State:** Living chapters recover the *essence* of each RFC and cross-link to the full text
  now in [`/rfcs`](../../../rfcs), instead of reproducing 2,000+ lines verbatim.
- **Why:** the RFCs are in-repo and are the source of truth; duplicating them invites drift.
- **Closes when:** n/a — this is a deliberate division of labour. For verbatim canon, read the
  RFCs directly; the book is the continuous lens over them.

### LG-3 · The Chronicle is a single-instrument reading 🔴
- **State:** the Chronicle (and most of the record it draws on) was written largely by one
  process (READING-001 §1).
- **Why:** survivorship bias built into the record by the relay mechanism.
- **Closes when:** X2 runs — an independent process reads the same record — and its divergences
  are folded into the Chronicle. Tracked in the [Chronicle Log's standing watch](../chronicle/CHRONICLE-LOG.md).

### LG-4 · The atlas-grade illustrations are still specified, not produced ⌫→🔵
- **State:** carried over from Recovery-Edition Gap G-8; the Living Edition adds Mermaid/text
  diagrams inline but no rich plates.
- **Closes when:** an illustrator produces them into `assets/` against the fixed frames.

## Part 3 — The Living-Book process (how the book stays alive)

When new canonical knowledge lands in the repository, run this loop (it mirrors the discipline
itself):

1. **Detect** — a new/updated RFC, a decided experiment, a journal event, a closed Q.
2. **Locate** — using the [Provenance Map](provenance-map.md), find affected chapters.
3. **Diff the doctrine** — does it *add*, *deepen*, or *supersede*? Supersession is additive:
   the old claim is struck (⌫) and kept, never deleted.
4. **Re-ink** — update the affected passages' medium (pencil→blue→black) and margins
   (green/red) to the new evidence; update the chapter maturity meter.
5. **Append the Chronicle** — add a dated, cited entry to
   [`CHRONICLE-LOG.md`](../chronicle/CHRONICLE-LOG.md); never rewrite earlier entries.
6. **Update the apparatus** — provenance map, glossary additions, this register.
7. **Record the update** — the edit is itself an event; ideally noted in the journal
   (`./ods note`), so the book's own evolution stays in the Record it describes.

**The non-erasure rule.** Never overwrite recovered doctrine to make room for new doctrine;
never collapse a historical sidebar when its conflict resolves; never raise a medium toward
black without a ratification to justify it. Ink is earned by survival, not by age.

**And the seal.** The [Recovery Edition](../../recovery-edition/) is never touched by this
loop. New knowledge continues the book here, in the Living Edition — never by editing the
sealed one.
