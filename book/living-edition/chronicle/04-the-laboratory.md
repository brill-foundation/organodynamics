# Chronicle · Act IV — The Laboratory

⏱ *what happened · the discipline becomes executable · events `d822ca8a` → `5532581f`*

```
Medium  ✏️──🔵──⚫   blue ink: for the first time, evidence — not argument — changes something
```

Act IV is the turn the whole project had been unable to make: from designing the discipline
to *running* it. It is the shortest act and the most important, because it contains the
first moment the discovery loop's reality-half actually executed.

## Commissioning the outside

The act opens with two acts of a *second* process — `claude-sonnet-5`, a different model
than the `claude-fable-5` that wrote most of the record — beginning to build the machinery
of independence:

- a **commissioning request** for two independent constitutional reviews of RFC-000, one
  blind and one contexted (`d822ca8a`), to advance META-REVIEW-000 toward quorum;
- the **X2 packet** (`ea2261c4`): a record manifest for an independent, non-Claude reading,
  *deliberately excluding* READING-001 and THEORY-001 so their findings could not leak into
  the replication meant to test them.

> 🟢 insight — this is the discipline protecting its own falsifiability with real
> engineering: to test whether READING-001's patterns are the *process* or one instrument's
> *style*, you must hand a fresh reader the record *without* the answer. The X2 packet is
> that discipline made into a file.

## The doorway, and the demand question

A **Doorway brief** (`76b0244d`) refocused Season 1 on *admission cost* — the recorded path
must become the path of least resistance — and the **SEASON-1-DOORWAY-PLAN** (`6fdc2a7d`)
made a sharp, falsifiable bet: Experiment A (mechanical friction) was *falsified before the
season began* — measured cost 0.134 seconds — so the real bottleneck is not friction but
**demand**: will anyone besides the first author ever write to the record? Experiment **D-0
— the first stranger** — was placed ahead of all friction work, with the registered
prediction that demand, not friction, is the likelier failure.

## The laboratory opens

Then the pivotal event (`eddbf397`): **the laboratory opened. `./ods`** — the smallest
environment that makes Season 1 executable: `setup`, `note` (the doorway: a thought → the
permanent record), `status` (the Season 1 instrument), `review-add`, and experiment-packet
assembly. Its first run was its own first end-to-end use of its own doorway. The discipline
now had a body.

> ⏱ **The loop's reality-half turns for the first time.** **EXPERIMENT-002 — lab
> calibration** (`5532581f`): three runs, three findings, three fixes, all retested. The
> cold-clone run found the doorway broke on fresh machines *and* that `ods` reported success
> on a failed commit — both real defects, both fixed. An induced-conflict run found that the
> lab's *own* guidance ("keep both lines") broke the hash chain, and that the verified
> resolution was different. As the journal records it, in the plainest words in the whole
> Record:
>
> > 🟢 *"First time the loop's reality half turned: a built artifact was staked, exposed,
> > falsified, and replaced, all in the record."*

That sentence is the hinge of the entire Chronicle. For three acts the discipline had
theorised a loop it had never closed — commit, expose, *and nothing had ever come back to
force a change by evidence.* In Act IV something finally did: the laboratory's own code was
wrong, reality said so, and the record shows the correction. Small, mechanical, unglamorous
— and the first true execution of the thing the whole project exists to do.

> 🔴 tension — honesty preserved: this was *self*-exposure by *one* process. D-0 (the first
> stranger) had still not happened; META-REVIEW-000 was still below quorum; the X2 lock was
> still closed. The loop turned, but on the cheapest possible material — the lab testing
> itself. The reality that answers *without negotiating* was still, mostly, waiting.
