# Chronicle · Act I — Genesis

⏱ *what happened · the founding week · commits `1be63ae` → `473f4a9`*

```
Medium  ✏️──🔵──⚫   this act is told in blue ink: the events are settled record
```

The project began not with the discipline but with the *shape* of a book. On 2026-07-06 the
repository was initialised (`1be63ae`) with a one-line vision — *an engineering discipline
for evolving representations of reality* — and nine empty chapter files whose titles alone
formed a table of contents: **The Door, Vision, Organodynamics, Come, Reality Layers,
Personal Worlds, Hospitality, The Sun, Open Questions.** No prose. A spine without a body.

Then, in four commits over the same day, the body began to grow — not as book prose but as
**RFCs**, and the discipline discovered its first ideas by writing them down and immediately
challenging them:

- `39fa24e` — **RFC-001 · The Instrument** opened: a measurement is a versioned,
  reproducible function from the Record to observations; measurement is never neutral.
- `e1ff5a6` — **RFC-001 rev 2**: a single instrument fails its own test. The **Observatory**
  is discovered — many instruments over one Record, disagreement juxtaposed never averaged.
- `c9dcb72` — **RFC-001 rev 3**: a deeper correction. Not the Record but **Reality** is the
  anchor — "the one node nothing else produces." And a deepening: *disagreement is
  information*, not merely a defence against Goodhart.
- `473f4a9` — **RFC-002 · Inquiry** opened: observation has two modes, and only the ambient
  one was defined; the directed mode (composing instruments around a live question) is named
  and left thin.

> 🟢 insight — the founding move of the whole project is visible already here: **the RFC is
> the first use of the mechanism it describes.** RFC-001 calls itself "a calibration run."
> The discipline is built by running itself on itself, in the open, keeping every revision.

This is the material the **[Recovery Edition](../../recovery-edition/)** reconstructs in
full, and its [Appendix C](../../recovery-edition/appendices/C-evolution-of-rfc-001.md)
charts the three revisions in detail. The Chronicle records only the shape of the week: a
book skeleton, then a discipline discovering — by challenge and revision — that its own
foundations were not where it first thought.

> ⏱ testimony — everything in Act I is *constitutive* record (RFC-001 §7): these commits are
> the acts they describe, and the Record cannot be wrong about them. From Act II onward, much
> of the Chronicle is *testimony* — events reporting conversations and external reviews that
> the record proves were reported, not that they were true. The marking begins where the
> uncertainty does.

At the close of Act I the project had a discipline in embryo (RFC-001, RFC-002) and an empty
book. What it did not yet have — and would spend the next acts discovering it needed — was a
way to govern its own development. That discovery opens Act II.
