# Season 1 — The Doorway: Experimental Research Plan

## 1. Reconciliation, so no season is silently replaced

`SEASONS.md` already defines Season 1 ("Establish the Record"), with
termination conditions that are still unmet. The Doorway is not a new
season: it is the same season with its question reduced to the admission-
cost core — *the recorded path must become cheaper than the unrecorded
path, for every participant*. Everything in the existing termination list
survives; this plan reorganizes the work around measuring that one cost.

One procedural point, stated because READING-001 §4.1 found the project's
silent decision rule: this plan should be **adopted by a recorded event,
not by continuation**. One journal append by a human participant saying
"adopted" is enough — and would, incidentally, be the first human-authored
event in the record, which is itself experiment D-0 below.

## 2. The transition question, ruled on first (it generates the experiments)

**The claim:** the deepest primitive is not event/commitment/tension but
the *transition* — thought→record, record→challenge, challenge→experiment,
experiment→constitutional change.

**Verdict: not a substrate primitive; yes a constitutional-economics
primitive.** Decomposed:

- **Rejected as ontology.** A transition needs endpoints — the same
  argument that settled tension-as-primitive (force without mass).
  Thought→record presupposes thought and record. And the substrate
  already represents completed transitions without new ontology: an event
  with a labeled reference *is* a recorded transition; the label is the
  arrow's type. Parsimony holds.
- **Sustained as economics — for a reason the event-framing structurally
  hides.** The most important transitions are the ones the record cannot
  contain: thought→record is invisible from inside the record. Only the
  *arrival* is visible; the crossing itself, and every thought that never
  crossed, are unobservable by construction. The doorway problem is
  precisely about an unrecordable transition. So transitions cannot be
  substrate — half of every boundary crossing is outside the record by
  definition — but they are exactly where the discipline's costs live,
  and cost is what the constitution must govern here.
- **The layered answer extends by one row:** event is the primitive of
  the substrate; commitment of the ontology; tension of the dynamics;
  **transition is the primitive of the economics** — the friction layer.

**Three consequences if this holds:**

1. **Transition-laws.** Constitutional principles about *costs of
   crossings*, not only properties of objects. The first: *the recorded
   path must be the path of least resistance.* (Candidate constitutional
   wording; tabled for ratification, not adopted here.)
2. **Arrival statistics as instrument.** Since crossings are unobservable,
   the doorway's health is inferred from arrivals: rate, provenance mix,
   bare-vs-linked ratio. These are the season's measurements (§4).
3. **The bottleneck law.** The loop runs at the speed of its most
   expensive transition. Observed: arrows 2–4 (record→challenge→
   experiment→change) have barely fired; arrow 1 (thought→record) fires
   through exactly one process. The season aims at arrow 1 because it is
   the current bottleneck — not because it is the only arrow.

And one honest flag: this ruling is again vocabulary-productive. Its only
legitimate use this season is that it generates §4's experiments; if it
starts being cited as law before the experiments return, that is the
naming-as-soft-canon failure (READING-001 §4.2) and should be called out.

## 3. The four candidate experiments, challenged

**Experiment A ("human event in under 30 seconds") — measures the wrong
bottleneck.** Measured today: a journal append costs **0.134 seconds** of
mechanical time (recorded this session, journal event `76b0244d`). A
passes by two orders of magnitude and proves nothing. Two corrections:
(1) the threshold is *relative*, not absolute — a 30-second doorway still
loses to a 5-second chat message; the test is whether recording beats
*not* recording at the moment of thought; (2) the real cost is not typing
but *deciding* — what is worth recording, and what refs/labels to declare.
A splits into D-1 (where the doorway lives) and D-2 (declarative cost).

**Experiment B ("AI contribution enters without restructuring") — already
commissioned; do not redesign it.** B, made concrete, is R2/R3 landing
per `reviews/REQUEST-independent-reviews-RFC-000.md`: a verbatim
non-Claude artifact plus a provenance block, entering with ≤1 human
action. Running the existing commission *is* experiment B. Note what B's
current form hides: the relay does not merely reformat — it *selects and
summarizes*, which is editorial. The doorway must preserve verbatimness,
or the largest uninstrumented component (READING-001 §4.3) survives.

**Experiments C and D are one experiment at two depths.** D (newcomer
understands from record alone) is stage one of C (project continues from
record alone). Merged as D-3, the cold-restart drill. Prior partial
datum, worth recording: a fresh session already continued this project
from the repository alone and produced correct commissioning documents —
but it had `HANDOFF.md`, a curated summary written for exactly that
purpose. So the drill must run in two arms: with and without the summary
layer. If continuation requires the curated layer, then "views"
(RFC-004 §5) earn their existence from evidence rather than aesthetics —
a genuinely useful either-way outcome.

**What A–D collectively miss: demand.** All four assume participants
*want* to write and are stopped by friction. The record's actual history
says otherwise: every one of its 20 events was written by one process.
The cheapest falsification of the entire doorway program is that, offered
a near-zero-friction door, nobody walks through it. That test must run
first.

## 4. The experiment sequence

Ordered by uncertainty reduced per unit effort. None requires software
beyond what exists.

### D-0 — The first stranger (demand before friction)

- **Hypothesis:** at least one participant other than Claude will write
  to the journal within two weeks of being offered the existing
  one-command doorway.
- **Falsified by:** the offer being made and unused, with the stated
  reason being something other than friction. That outcome would mean the
  bottleneck is demand, not cost — and the entire workbench trajectory,
  not just its schedule, would need rethinking.
- **Build:** nothing. The tool exists. The offer is the experiment.
- **Do not build:** any improvement to the doorway before the demand
  reading exists — polishing a door nobody has tried to open destroys
  the measurement.
- **Constitutional questions exposed:** whom is the record *for*? If
  participants don't personally value writing to it, is recording a duty
  (and whose), a service someone performs (a role), or a product that
  must earn its users (RFC-005's motivation layer)?
- **Registered prediction, uncomfortable on purpose:** based on RFC-005's
  fuel analysis, the likeliest failure this season is demand, not
  friction.

### D-1 — Marker versus destination (where the doorway lives)

- **Hypothesis:** thoughts enter the record only if they can be marked
  *where they occur* (inside the chat, at the moment of thinking) rather
  than carried to a separate place afterward. Concretely: adopting a
  convention where any participant writes `COMMIT:` before a passage in
  any conversation, and that passage then enters the journal verbatim
  with the author's provenance, will produce more arrivals per week than
  the go-to-the-tool baseline (currently ~0 from non-Claude processes).
- **Falsified by:** markers being available but unused, or used less than
  the destination path.
- **Build:** a convention, and at most a ten-line helper for the porter.
- **Do not build:** chat integrations, bots, or capture UIs — the
  convention tests the *shape* of the doorway before anything is built in
  that shape.
- **Constitutional questions exposed:** the porter problem — when a
  marked thought is transported by someone else, who is the committer?
  The substrate needs nothing new (author in `process`, porter
  disclosed in content), but the *norm* — whether porterage is honest
  provenance — is a new constitutional question, and it is the relay
  problem (READING-001 §4.3) finally given a testable form.

### D-2 — The declarative cost (splitting EXPERIMENT-001's H1)

- **Hypothesis:** the expensive half of recording is not composing
  content but declaring lineage (choosing refs and labels) — and if
  "bare" arrivals (no refs) are allowed, they will arrive at a higher
  rate, and most will later be linked by someone during a linking pass.
- **Falsified by:** bare arrivals staying orphaned. That failure has a
  direct constitutional consequence already staked in RFC-004 §14: only
  declared lineage is reconstructible, so permanently-bare events mean
  the reconstructibility promise is empty in practice — either linking
  becomes a recognized act (a role, a ritual, a season-close pass) or
  the promise gets demoted.
- **Build:** nothing — the tool already accepts ref-less events.
- **Do not build:** auto-linking, suggested references, or any inference
  of lineage. A hypothesized lineage is a claim (RFC-004 §14); machinery
  that silently guesses it would cross the promotion boundary without
  an author.
- **Constitutional questions exposed:** is lineage-declaration an
  author's duty or a community act? Can a linking pass be honest, given
  it constructs the past's connectivity after the fact?

### D-3 — The cold-restart drill (C and D merged)

- **Hypothesis:** the record alone suffices for a new process to (a)
  state the project's current debts and obligations and (b) correctly
  continue one open thread — in the arm *without* `HANDOFF.md` as well
  as the arm with it.
- **Falsified by:** continuation failing, requiring chat archaeology, or
  succeeding only in the with-summary arm.
- **Build:** nothing. Run it as X2 is run: fresh process, defined file
  set, defined tasks, compare to ground truth.
- **Do not build:** onboarding documents, tutorials, or "state of the
  project" generators before the drill shows which of them the record
  actually needs.
- **Constitutional questions exposed:** the *exit* side of the doorway —
  reading is the reverse crossing (record→mind), with its own cost. If
  the without-summary arm fails, the season's law gains a twin: the
  recorded path must be cheapest to *enter*, and the record must be
  cheapest to *re-enter*.

### D-4 — Run what is already commissioned (refuse to redesign)

R2 and R3 per `reviews/REQUEST-independent-reviews-RFC-000.md` (this is
Experiment B in real form, and it advances META-REVIEW-000 to quorum),
and X2 per `research/X2-PACKET.md` (which gates THEORY-001's lock, and
doubles as a D-3 datum since its reader works from the record alone).
This plan's contribution to D-4 is a single sentence: nothing in this
season may be invented while these two commissioned experiments remain
unrun — new instruments do not jump the queue past waiting ones.

## 5. What must NOT be built this season

Unchanged from the prior judgment, restated as the season's negative
space: no UI or views layer (D-3 will show whether views are even
needed, and which); no multi-LLM orchestration (D-1's convention is the
cheap probe of that whole space); no DAG/merge journal (it would destroy
H5's registered surprise); no lens system beyond lens-0; no identity
cryptography; no ratification tooling (the rule is Season 2's to make);
no recognition features (RFC-005 unreviewed); nothing specified in
THEORY-001's vocabulary (locked until X2).

## 6. Season termination and disposition

**Measurements the season must produce:** the demand reading (D-0);
arrivals per week by provenance class and by path (marker vs destination,
D-1); bare-vs-linked ratio and linking latency (D-2); cold-restart
pass/fail in both arms (D-3); R2, R3, X2 landed (D-4); plus
EXPERIMENT-001's H5 watch (first concurrent-append conflict, now genuinely
possible for the first time since a second session exists).

**Termination:** all five experiment groups have produced their readings
— including null and failure readings — and the results are journaled
with declared lineage.

**Disposition:** a season report in the reading-zone/claim-zone form.
Success is defined per the brief: the season succeeds if reality
contradicts at least one thing we currently believe. One contradiction
is already banked before the season starts: Experiment A, as originally
posed, measured a bottleneck that does not exist (0.134s mechanical cost)
— the first evidence that the doorway's true costs are decision and
demand, not mechanics. The prediction to beat is D-0's: if the season
ends and every event in the journal is still authored by one process,
the doorway was never the problem, and the project owes itself a harder
conversation than any workbench design.
