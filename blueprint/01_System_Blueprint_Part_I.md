## Preface
This document is the engineering blueprint for the first Canonical Reference Implementation of the
Organodynamics platform.
It is not part of the Constitution.
It does not define truth.
It does not replace any constitutional specification.
Instead, it translates the constitutional corpus into an executable engineering program.
Its audience is:
AI engineering systems
software architects
implementation teams
reviewers
future maintainers
The purpose of this document is to ensure that independent engineering teams build substantially
equivalent systems while preserving the constitutional intent of Organodynamics.
Whenever this Blueprint conflicts with the constitutional corpus, the constitutional corpus always
prevails.
## 1. Mission
The mission of the Canonical Reference Implementation is not to build an application.
It is to build the first working embodiment of the Organodynamics Constitution.

## 3. Engineering Mission
The engineering team SHALL construct a system capable of:
representing constitutional knowledge,
preserving provenance,
recording discovery,
supporting constitutional evolution,
maintaining semantic integrity,
remaining fully explainable.
The system SHALL never optimize for convenience at the expense of constitutional traceability.

The Constitution is the product.

## 4.2 Explainability Before Automation
Every output produced by the system SHALL be explainable.
No component may become a black box within the constitutional workflow.
## 4.3 Evolution Before Optimization
The first implementation is expected to evolve.
Architectural flexibility is therefore more valuable than premature optimization.
## 4.4 Explicit Before Implicit
Every important relationship SHALL be represented explicitly.
Avoid hidden assumptions.
Avoid implicit state.
Avoid invisible coupling.
## 4.5 Knowledge Before Interface
The knowledge model is the product.
The user interface is one possible representation of that model.
The UI SHALL never become the architectural center of the system.
