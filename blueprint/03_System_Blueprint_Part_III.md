

## Organodynamics System Blueprint
Part III — Engineering Execution Plan
## 21. Engineering Philosophy
The purpose of the engineering team is not to invent Organodynamics.
The purpose is to realize the constitutional corpus faithfully.
Engineering SHALL maximize constitutional fidelity before implementation convenience.
Whenever uncertainty exists, the engineering team SHALL preserve the Constitution and record
implementation uncertainty through the Discovery Methodology.
- Definition of Success
The first Canonical Reference Implementation SHALL be considered successful when:
every constitutional artifact has a canonical representation,
every constitutional relationship is traceable,
every change is historically attributable,
every discovery follows the Discovery Lifecycle,
every constitutional decision is explainable,
the complete system remains independently reproducible.
Feature count SHALL NOT be used as the primary measure of success.
## 23. Engineering Phases
Implementation SHALL proceed through constitutional milestones.
A milestone SHALL not begin until the previous milestone satisfies its acceptance criteria.

## 24. Engineering Rules
The engineering team SHALL obey the following rules.
## Rule 1
Never optimize before constitutional correctness.
## Rule 2
Never duplicate constitutional logic.
## Rule 3
Every important decision SHALL be documented.
## 5

## Rule 4
Every architectural tension SHALL become an RFC.
Never hide architectural uncertainty inside code.
## Rule 5
Delete complexity before adding functionality.
## Rule 6
Prefer explicit models over implicit behavior.
## Rule 7
Every subsystem SHALL remain independently testable.
## Rule 8
Every constitutional object SHALL possess stable identity.
## Rule 9
Every important action SHALL remain historically reconstructable.
## Rule 10
No implementation shortcut may violate constitutional principles.
## 25. Decision Protocol
Whenever engineers encounter uncertainty, they SHALL ask the following questions in order:
Does the Constitution already answer this?1.

Does the Architecture constrain it?
Does the Implementation Specification define it?
Does the Glossary clarify it?
Does the Discovery Methodology prescribe how to resolve it?
If all answers remain insufficient:
Create an RFC.
Continue with the least constitutionally disruptive implementation.
## 26. Completion Criteria
Version 1 SHALL be considered complete when:
the constitutional corpus is faithfully represented,
the Discovery Lifecycle is operational,
constitutional governance functions correctly,
historical traceability is complete,
semantic integrity is preserved,
independent reviewers can reproduce equivalent constitutional behavior.
Completion SHALL be measured by constitutional fidelity.
Not by application size.
## Engineering Principle
The first implementation is not expected to be the most powerful implementation.
It is expected to be the most faithful one.
Future systems may become faster,
larger,
more intelligent,
and more autonomous.
