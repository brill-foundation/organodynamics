# The Constitutional Map of Organodynamics

*A single structural view of the whole discipline — how every corpus family,
principle, protocol, RFC, and CAB stands in relation to the others. Ordered by
structure, not by date.*

This map is itself a **derived reading** (charter P3), not law. It is a Camera
onto the corpus: it stores no new truth, and if it ever disagrees with the
documents it points at, they are right and it is stale. Read it to orient, then
read the sources.

---

## I. The one picture

The discipline is a single column bounded by two membranes. Reality sits above
it and votes; interfaces sit below it and look. Everything between is one
append-only line of committed understanding, and every layer is *derived from*
or *reduced from* the layer above it — never a second source of truth.

```
                     REALITY  —  owns substance; votes by evidence; the final word
                        │        (RFC-002 §4: no authority layer above the process)
  ══════════════════════╪══════════════════  ADMISSION  (upper membrane)
                        ▼        a participant, with provenance, commits Reality inward
   THE RECEIVED CORPUS        constitution/   (imported, read-only)
     OCS  what a constitution IS      OAS  how it is structured
     ODM  how it evolves (lifecycle)  OGD  what its words mean
     OIS  how it is implemented
                        │   reduced to its smallest enforceable core ↓
                        ▼
   THE PROTOCOL  ·  FROZEN (CAB-008)  ·  resting on the Axioms
     Identity · Grounding · Provenance · Explicit Judgment · Sealing
                        │   inhabited; reality surfaces principles while living here ↓
                        ▼
   THE GROWN CHARTER
     CONSTITUTION.md §1–§8a  ·  Stewardship Charter (CAB-005: P1–P5, the 3 tests)  ·  CAB-012
                        │   executed as the smallest machine that can hold it ↓
                        ▼
   THE KERNEL
     11 event types · derived state · tools (lab · conformance · report · habitat)
                        │   reasoned about, mandated, remembered ↓
                        ▼
   THE LIVING RECORD & REASONING
     the append-only Record   ·   RFC-001 … RFC-008   ·   CAB-001 … CAB-013
                        ▼
  ══════════════════════╤══════════════════  NON-AUTHORITATIVE RENDERING  (lower membrane)
                        ▼        a participant acts THROUGH a Camera that holds no truth
   HABITATS  ·  CAMERAS
     the Door · the Table · the Terminal · (the Spatial Habitat, future)
```

The two membranes are the same rule at opposite ends (RFC-008): **a participant
acts *through*; a surface never acts *as*.** Admission forbids Reality from
entering unauthored; the renderer boundary forbids an interface from authoring.

## II. The seven strata

Each layer is named by what it answers, its **temperature** (fixed or living),
and who may write it.

| Stratum | Answers | Temperature | Who writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reality** | what is true / apt | — | no one writes reality; it votes |
| **Received Corpus** (constitution/) | what the discipline received | frozen · read-only | imported; changes only via ODM lifecycle |
| **The Protocol** | the minimum every participant owes | **FROZEN** (CAB-008) | no one — reduction is complete |
| **The Grown Charter** | what the Laboratory learned about itself | living, but load-bearing | reality, surfacing principles; recorded |
| **The Kernel** | the law made executable | living; replaceable | engineers, under the charter |
| **Record & Reasoning** | what happened, and why we concluded it | append-only; ever-growing | any registered participant |
| **Habitats / Cameras** | how the Laboratory is inhabited | freely replaceable | anyone; but they author no truth |

**The received corpus, by family** (five questions, one discipline):

- **OCS — the Constitutional Spec.** *What a constitution is.* The Alphabet
  (OCS-000, the primitives), Grammar, Logic, Ontology, Epistemology, Truth,
  Continuity. The theory.
- **OAS — the Architecture Spec.** *How it is structured.* Identity, Place,
  Memory, Event, Time, Reasoning, Decision, Trust, Federation. The shape.
- **ODM — the Discovery Methodology.** *How it evolves.* Observation →
  Discovery → Review → Integration (the lifecycle RFC-004 §4.1 names as the
  only legal path to constitutional change). The motion.
- **OGD — the Glossary.** *What the words mean.* Terminology, Canonical Naming,
  Concept Authority, Provenance. The language layer (§2: implementation names
  must never enter the world's language).
- **OIS — the Implementation Spec.** *How it is built.* Runtime, Engines,
  Conformance Verification, Service Contracts. **The Kernel is the reference
  implementation OF OIS** (RFC-004: "the first convergent implementation").

## III. The five spines — how the strata are one system

The map's real content is not the layers but the **spines**: principles that
pierce every stratum, appearing under different names at different altitudes.
Each spine is *one* principle, recognized repeatedly. This is where "how they
relate" actually lives.

### Spine 1 — One source of truth
*There is exactly one committed line, and everything else is derived from it.*

```
Axiom 3  history is superseded, never replaced   (the append-only Record)
   └ P3  store events & authored content; derive everything else
       └ RFC-005  Laboratory State / reports are derived Cameras, never stored state
           └ RFC-008  renderers are non-authoritative; no interface holds truth
               └ the habitat guard  refuses a second live tip (one Record, one line)
```

### Spine 2 — No authority above the process
*No one rules; reality votes; the machine checks only form.*

```
RFC-002 §4  inquiry is not an authority layer
   └ CAB-005  the steward is a seat, not an authority
       └ session 30–31  the steward is an INTENTION, not a standing office
           └ session 29  the machine certifies FORM; reality owns SUBSTANCE
               └ RFC-007  reconciliation ADOPTS a lineage; it never rules one invalid
                   └ RFC-008  a renderer originates acts but authors no truth
```

### Spine 3 — The arrow of justification
*The ground is committed before, and independently of, the grounded — so
justification can never be rationalization.*

```
Obl. 2 Grounding      a claim declares its basis before it stands
Obl. 4 Explicit Judgment  a conclusion states its reasoning, never a side effect
   └ P9–P11  observation is prior to interpretation
       └ RFC-005  conditions before recommendations
           └ RFC-007 §3.2  continuity declared before the lineage is judged
               └ RFC-008  a concept is authored into the model before any Camera renders it
```

### Spine 4 — Growth by recognition, not invention
*New capability is almost always old law seen at a new scope.*

```
P1  open sets, closed disciplines        (freedom in the data, stability in the invariants)
P5  growth by addition                   (new rooms are folders; new events extend an open log)
   └ the meta-principle:  discovery is recognition
       └ RFC-008  every principle tagged RECOGNIZED / GENERALIZED / NEW (only 2 of ~13 are NEW)
           └ this map:  the spines exist because the same principle kept reappearing
```

### Spine 5 — The two membranes (what crosses in, what may render out)
*The Record is curated Reality; interfaces are non-authoritative Cameras.*

```
ADMISSION (in):  Pencil→Ink (CAB-004/005)  =  surfacing L1→L2 (rotating-table §3)
                 =  provenance / requireRegistered (Obl. 1 + 3)         ── one boundary, three names
      └ the Record is curated Reality, not Reality  ("if it's not in the record, it didn't happen")
RENDERING (out): the Door is "a Camera, not the Place"  →  RFC-008 renderer boundary
```

## IV. The authority gradient

Who may do what — the discipline's whole answer to power:

```
REALITY            decides SUBSTANCE (is this true / apt / continuing?)      — votes, does not rule
   │
THE STEWARD        guards INVARIANTS, exposes conflict, refuses premature    — a seat/intention,
(Reduction seat)   encoding; RULES NOTHING                                     never an authority
   │
THE MACHINE        certifies FORM only (presence, reference, order, chain)   — cannot touch substance
```

Session 29 is the hinge: the machine can enforce *order and separation* (form)
but never *truth* (substance) — which is precisely why the discipline's
strongest tools are all orderings (append-only, grounding-declared,
seal-binds-chain, admission-before-derivation), and why substance is left,
structurally, to reality.

## V. The frozen line

What cannot change, and what lives — the single most important distinction for a
newcomer:

- **Frozen / read-only:** the received corpus (constitution/), and **the
  Protocol's five obligations** (CAB-008 — "the reduction is complete"; a sixth,
  Continuity, was eliminated as derivable from the five). Changing these is
  reality's act, through the ODM lifecycle only (RFC-004 §4.1).
- **Living:** the charter (reality surfaces new §-clauses), the kernel
  (replaceable implementations), the Record (append-only, ever-growing), the
  RFCs (all currently `open`), the habitats.

Continuity is the seam: it is *derived* (Identity + Provenance + Sealing over an
append-only chain), which is why it is not a sixth obligation, and why the whole
column can grow without the frozen core moving.

## VII. Where the system is under tension right now

A map should show its live edges, not only its settled structure:

- **The fork.** The Record is divergent — an 88-event lineage and a 156-event
  lineage, sharing #0–#83. Every write is refused until reconciled (RFC-007).
  This is the one place the "one source of truth" spine is currently strained.
- **The queue behind the fork.** RFC-005/006/007/008 and CAB-013 have reasoning
  but no Record anchor yet; they wait on the first reconciliation. The first
  act that moves them is the steward's **continuity declaration** (RFC-007 §3.2).
- **Open constitutional clauses** awaiting reality's vote: §7 (Screen = Camera —
  RFC-008 proposes a reading but cannot ratify it); §8/§8a (sovereignty vs the
  larger world); the medium-vs-maturity question (CAB-004); the rotating-table
  membrane questions (§7.A what crosses, §7.B who authorizes the crossing).
- **Standing unknowns** (the Record's own agenda): whether the five obligations
  have authoritative definitions; whether a stranger can arrive by the Kit
  alone; whether a website visitor is an Observer.
