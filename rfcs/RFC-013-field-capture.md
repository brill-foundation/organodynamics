# RFC-013 — Field Capture

**Epistemic status.** Pencil, not Ink; one mortal instance, forked branch, unsealable
here (RFC-007); imports nothing; holds no authority. Its assigned job is **aggressive
falsification**: to try, in good faith and as hard as possible, to collapse "Field
Capture" into something the corpus already has, and to report survival only for what
resists every collapse. It applies the corpus's own decisive test — the **reversed
burden** of the frozen Protocol (§1). Reality remains the only authority.

## 0. The claim under test, and the protocol

**Field Capture, as proposed:** a canonical observational record that (i) exists *before*
any particular representation, (ii) preserves *observable experience*, (iii) is the source
from which many renderings derive (photograph, illustration, film, simulation, narrative,
audio, virtual environment), and (iv) does not change while the renderings change. Offered
as distinct from prompt, image, rendering, Camera, and Membrane; paired against **Brill**
("preserving semantic meaning") as its complement ("preserving observable experience").

**Falsification protocol.** For each claimed property, name the existing corpus object it
maps to and attempt the collapse. Field Capture is a genuinely new canonical object **only
if** at least one property resists every collapse **and** clears the frozen Protocol's
reversed burden (§1). Otherwise, per the mandate, the existing terminology is adopted.

## 1. The gate: the frozen Protocol's reversed burden (CAB-008)

The Protocol is **FROZEN**: "*no new … architectural layers, no new abstractions — unless
implementation produces explicit engineering evidence that this protocol is insufficient.
The burden of proof is reversed: implementation must justify architectural change.*" A new
canonical object *is* a new abstraction. So the question is not "is Field Capture a
plausible object?" but "**has implementation hit a wall the existing objects cannot
hold?**" The image work is the implementation; the evidence of insufficiency, if any, must
come from it. Nothing in the corpus records such a wall. Field Capture therefore starts
**behind** the gate: it must earn novelty against a standing presumption of sufficiency,
not merely assert it.

## 2. The collapses (run aggressively)

**2.1 "A canonical observational record" → the OBSERVATION, via deposit-with-observation.**
The corpus's grounding vocabulary already names it: an assertion is grounded in "an
**observation** in the record, a cited **source**, a **derivation** … or … **conjecture**"
(Protocol, obligation 2), and "**observation is separate from, and prior to,
interpretation**" (P-009/010/011). RFC-006 §4 names the very pattern — "**the
deposit-with-observation pattern for testimony**" — as belonging to Organodynamics. A
record that is observational and prior to interpretation is an **OBSERVATION** (or a cited
**source**). **Collapses.**

**2.2 "Exists before any representation" → incoherent; the honest version is P3's stored
master.** By RFC-012's own I10 (information is physical; a representation acquires content
only by admitting a realization), *a capture is already a representation*: a documentary
photograph is a 2-D visual projection that has already discarded sound, depth, and smell;
a field recording has already chosen audio. There is no pre-representational preserved
"experience." The coherent claim is not "before any representation" but "the **primary /
master** representation, from which derivatives are made" — and master-vs-derivative is
exactly **P3** ("store events & authored content; **derive everything else**"). **Collapses
into P3.**

**2.3 "Many renderings derive from one; the rendering changes, the capture does not" →
P3 + Axiom 3 + the Camera (RFC-005/008).** This is the corpus's founding architecture, not
a new object: the stored record is immutable (**Axiom 3**) and authoritative; every
rendering is a **non-authoritative Camera / derived reading** of it (RFC-005), and "**one
Laboratory, many Cameras**" is RFC-008's whole thesis. "The Field Capture does not change;
the rendering changes" is P3 + Axiom 3 restated. **Collapses.**

**2.4 "Preserves observable experience" → Brill-content (RFC-006 §4).** The corpus already
splits, on the RFC-004 seam, the **grammar** (Organodynamics — the lens, the Camera axis,
the deposit-with-observation pattern) from the **lived content** (**Brill** — "journals,
conversations, lived days, and the Garden"). "Observable experience" is precisely the
**Brill** side; "the discipline ships the lens; the life stays with the instance." So Field
Capture ≈ **Brill-content deposited through the Organodynamics grammar** — not a new object.
Note the **inversion**: the proposal pairs "Brill = semantic meaning" against "Field Capture
= observable experience," but the corpus uses Brill for the *lived/observable content* and
Organodynamics for the *semantic grammar*. The proposal has the corpus's own axis backwards
— itself evidence that Field Capture is a **re-derivation of the existing
Organodynamics/Brill seam under new names**. **Collapses.**

**2.5 The name "capture" → the corpus already rejected it in favor of "deposit."** RFC-006's
kept-rejection #4: "**Automatic capture of conversations** — deposit is a chosen act with
provenance (passivity clause); testimony enters by intention, **never by ambient
recording**." The Protocol's **passivity clause** forbids the Laboratory any initiative to
capture. So a *chosen, provenanced* Field Capture simply **is a deposit** (existing term);
an *ambient* one is the thing the corpus deliberately refused. Either way the word "capture"
**collapses** into "deposit," or reintroduces a rejected abstraction.

## 3. What survives the collapses?

**No object survives.** Every property of Field Capture maps onto: the OBSERVATION / cited
source (2.1), P3's stored master (2.2), Axiom 3 + the non-authoritative Camera (2.3),
Brill-content (2.4), and the chosen **deposit** (2.5).

**One thing resists — but it is a *mode*, not an object.** The corpus's grounding vocabulary
(observation / source / derivation / conjecture) does not *distinguish by payload kind*
between:
- a **semantic-proposition** observation — "I observed a red sunset at 18:00" (a described
  perception), and
- a **primary-perceptual-artifact** observation — the preserved master medium itself (the
  camera RAW, the field recording) designated canonical and prior, from which other-medium
  renderings are derived.

Both are *observations / sources* under the existing grammar; the corpus simply has no
*name* for the second **mode**. That is a real under-specification — but it is a **typing
distinction inside the existing OBSERVATION/source primitives**, not a new canonical object,
and not a new layer. It is the same residue RFC-012 §5 flagged (artifacts as first-class;
the observation/source under-typing), one level more concrete.

## 4. Does the residue clear the gate?

Per §1, the perceptual-artifact *mode* earns canonical naming **only if** the image-work
implementation has produced explicit evidence that the undifferentiated "observation /
source" is **insufficient** in practice — e.g., that renderings across media provably need
a typed master the current grammar cannot express without ambiguity. That evidence is the
implementer's (reality's) to present; it is not in the corpus. **Protected, not decided.**
And if it is presented, the honest form is a **recognized mode of observation-as-primary-
artifact** — a typing that fills the existing slot — **never** a new object named "Field
Capture," and never a new layer (the frozen Protocol forbids both without the evidence).

## 5. The verdict

**Field Capture does not survive falsification as a new canonical object.** Its every
property collapses into objects the corpus already has — the deposited **OBSERVATION**
(RFC-006 §4), the stored master under **P3** and **Axiom 3**, the non-authoritative
**Camera** (RFC-005/008), and **Brill-content** (RFC-006 §4) — and its name reintroduces
the "ambient capture" the corpus deliberately rejected in favor of **deposit** (RFC-006
kept-rejection #4; the passivity clause). Per the mandate's own rule — *if it collapses,
adopt the existing terminology* — the recommendation is to say **"a deposited observation
(Brill-content), rendered by non-authoritative Cameras,"** and to retire "Field Capture" as
a canonical object.

The genuine residue is not an object but an **under-named mode**: observation-as-primary-
perceptual-artifact, distinct from observation-as-semantic-proposition. Whether to name it
is gated by the frozen Protocol's reversed burden and is **reality's to decide on
implementation evidence** — and even then it fills an existing slot rather than adding one.

The deflating, honoring one-liner: **you have not found a missing object; you have
re-derived the Observation, the Camera, and the Organodynamics/Brill seam from the image
work — which is strong corroboration that the existing grammar already reaches the place the
image work arrived at.**

## 6. Kept rejections, and the non-authority self-limit

1. **Minting "Field Capture" as a new canonical object** — rejected (§2 collapses; §1 gate
   unmet).
2. **The term "capture"** — rejected; the corpus chose "deposit" (chosen, provenanced), and
   rejected ambient capture (RFC-006 #4; passivity clause).
3. **"Brill = semantic meaning"** — rejected; it contradicts the corpus, where Brill is the
   *lived/observable content* and Organodynamics the *semantic grammar* (RFC-006 §4).
4. **Elevating the perceptual-artifact mode without implementation evidence** — refused; the
   frozen Protocol's reversed burden governs, and the evidence is reality's to present.
5. **Treating this RFC as authority** — impossible and undesired: open Pencil, a Camera,
   unsealable here, authored by an instance that will not return. Cite it to challenge.
