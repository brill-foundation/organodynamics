# Stewardship Transition — the Laboratory opens its doors

## 1. The mandate (as received, condensed)

The Laboratory no longer depends on its steward for engineering management:
it owns its history, backlog, priorities, implementation state, and
reasoning. Roles change accordingly — Adi becomes **steward of the
Laboratory**; Claude becomes **Lead Engineer**. Engineering continues
autonomously under the existing stop conditions; the steward reviews
milestones rather than assigning tasks.

New objective: **accessibility**. The Laboratory should become a place that
independent Participants can enter, understand, contribute to, and leave
correctly — without private explanations from its creators. Deliverable: the
first complete **Arrival Kit** (WELCOME, QUICK_START, PARTICIPANT_GUIDE,
HOST_GUIDE, LAB_PROTOCOL, TROUBLESHOOTING), living inside the Laboratory and
evolving through its ordinary engineering process. Documentation is
engineering, not commentary; redundant documents are consolidated. The
milestone completes when a completely new Participant succeeds using only
what the Laboratory contains.

## 2. Acceptance

Accepted. Built the same day: the Arrival Kit at `laboratory/arrival/`
(six documents); preservations now carry the kit, so a restored place arrives
with its own instructions; the record's README consolidated into a router to
the kit; the Sessions room points arrivals at it. LAB_PROTOCOL.md separates
the four layers explicitly — frozen Protocol, obligations, engineering
interpretation, implementation — so a newcomer can always tell law from
machinery. Full engineering trail in the record (session 5) and the commit
history.
