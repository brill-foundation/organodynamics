# Architectural Review — the Ecological Threshold

*Requested as an architectural evaluation, not implementation: is the current
substrate still the right foundation for the "Living Knowledge Ecosystem"
model? Held as a Reading; nothing was integrated.*

## Verdict

The substrate survives. Phase Shift 1 (Habitats, Geography/Ecology/Climate, the
Living Tree, Discovery Ecology) is roughly 80% the existing ontology wearing new
vocabulary and 20% genuinely new — and the new 20% (**decay, tempo,
process-discovery**) extends the substrate without breaking it. Phase Shift 2
(Preservation Metabolism) is legal under the constitution's own case law,
except one layer that would violate it if automated as proposed.

## The mapping (evidence for the verdict)

Geography = the object topology; Ecology = confidence/lifecycle; Climate = the
Reality anchor (RFC-001 §7); growth rings = the shipped save-count maturation
(reality already voted); pollinating = exchange of Preservations (§8a);
canonizing = the membrane. Only three items map to nothing: forgetting/compost,
a spectrum of tempos, and the discovery of processes.

## Key rulings

1. **Decay is reading-level, not record-level.** Compost = loss of authority
   (nothing may cite it; it feeds new growth), never loss of memory. The gen-2
   bell rings only if reality ever demands true erasure.
2. **The deepest question refutes the most elaborate proposal.** "Processes may
   be observer-drawn boundaries in continuous flows" dissolves the predefined
   7-stage Discovery Ecology pipeline eleven paragraphs above it. Processes,
   like Instruments, must be commissioned by challenge — never predeclared.
3. **The Tree contradicts the Ecosystem.** One trunk vs. many sovereign
   organisms. Keep the tree as rendering vocabulary; reject it as computation.
   If the world needs a plant metaphor, it is a forest.
4. **Preservation Metabolism**: Layers 1–2 (commit/push; sovereign artifact +
   smoke test) had completed the Manual → Repeated → Candidate lifecycle and
   are legal to automate. Layer 3 (the Discovery Snapshot) contains *claims* —
   claims are authored; furniture must never fill in the understanding, only
   hand over the empty page. The Snapshot is a pressed Journal page with
   provenance — existing furniture used reflexively.
5. **The trigger is a judgment.** "Every significant milestone" — who judges
   significant? Automatic after human declaration, never automatic judgment.
   (The pedal question, recurring.)

## The four load-bearing boundaries (reaffirmed)

Reality / Representation · Forge / Kernel · Furniture / Inhabitants ·
Place / Camera. Geography/Ecology/Climate are descriptions, not walls. The
gen-2 seed to watch: **furniture commissioning** — a second-generation
laboratory would let inhabitants commission furniture by challenge.
