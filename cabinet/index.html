✎ A local note — what an artifact is

An artifact is a research conversation preserved as it was, with metadata its author declared. The drawers darken with the true age of what they hold. The Cabinet remembers; it never interprets, summarizes, or ranks. The medium here is ink — kept exactly as deposited; certainty belongs to each author, not to the room.
