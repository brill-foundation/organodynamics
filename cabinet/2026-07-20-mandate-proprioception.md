# Mandate — Laboratory Status Reporting (Proprioception)

## 1. The mandate (as received, condensed)

The Laboratory should be able to generate a complete and objective snapshot
of its current state at any time — based on actual state, not conversational
memory; verified facts separated from observations, assumptions, and
recommendations; a permanent capability, not a one-time report; possibly with
a historical archive so future stewards can understand how the Laboratory
evolved. The proposal was explicitly offered for architectural review, not
literal implementation: improve it, challenge it, simplify it, and document
constitutional questions as RFCs rather than resolving them silently.

## 2. Acceptance, and what changed under review

Accepted, with three deliberate deviations from the initial vision, each
grounded in the corpus (full reasoning and kept rejections in RFC-005):

1. **"Risks / Recommendations / Executive summary" are not machine output.**
   The furniture is intentionally unintelligent; the machine certifies form,
   never substance (session 29). The report detects mechanical *conditions*;
   meaning and advice stay with Participants as recorded judgments.
2. **The report is a derived Camera, never stored state.** Regenerated on
   request (`node tools/report.js`), side-effect-free, safe to delete —
   charter P3. There is no maintained STATUS file to drift.
3. **The archive is an act, not a habit.** `--keep` requires an actor and an
   intention, passes the same habitat guard as every write, and records an
   observation pointing at the kept snapshot. Justified because *condition*
   (unlike history) is not replayable later; constrained because automatic
   archiving would violate the passivity clause.

## 3. Honest residue

This deposit was made while the working habitat carries a forked record
(the guard correctly refuses writes there). The Cabinet is authored files —
legal to extend — but the Record-side acknowledgment of this mandate must
wait for reconciliation, and is deliberately left to that moment rather than
forced through a side door.
