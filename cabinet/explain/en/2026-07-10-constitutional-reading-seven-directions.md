# What this note is, in plain language

Early in the project a batch of research ideas came in. This note sorts them using one
honest filter: which ideas are genuinely new building-blocks, and which are just nicer
names for things the project already had?

Its verdicts, roughly:

- **Already there, just renamed.** Some ideas — like the lab quietly "aging" while you're
  away — were already built weeks earlier. They didn't need adding, only better words.
- **Genuinely new, handle with care.** A few real newcomers were set aside for careful
  work: the idea of the lab having *a life of its own* (acting on its own), and the
  **"Butterfly"** — the ongoing human-and-AI partnership treated as the project's real
  author. Important rule: you must never reduce that partnership to a score; a scored
  partnership dies.
- **Arrived too early.** One idea (whole *communities* of labs) was judged to have shown
  up before reality was ready for it, so it was parked until such communities actually
  exist.

The deeper pattern it notices: the mature ideas are the system describing *itself*; the
genuinely new ones are all about the lab gaining life that isn't its owner's. It's a
record of careful sorting — keeping what's real, resisting what's premature.
