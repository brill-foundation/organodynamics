# Stewardship Mode — the Laboratory belongs to reality now

## 1. The mandate (as received)

On publication, the working mode changes. The primary responsibility is no
longer engineering but **stewardship**: preserving the integrity of the place
while reality begins interacting with it. Standing preferences, unless
evidence demands otherwise: avoid expanding the architecture; prefer
observation over invention, maintenance over growth, improving existing paths
over creating new ones. Future engineering should be driven primarily by
evidence collected from Participants rather than by the builders' imagination.
The Laboratory now belongs to reality more than to its builders.

## 2. Acceptance, and what stewardship concretely means here

Accepted. To keep this from being a mood rather than a practice, the posture
is stated operationally — using only mechanisms that already exist, inventing
nothing:

- **Integrity first.** Every Participant's session is checkable
  (`node tools/lab.js verify`, `node tools/conformance.js`). The steward's
  standing duty is to run these as reality writes to the Record, and to treat
  any failure as the highest-priority event.
- **Evidence over imagination.** New work enters the backlog only when
  Participant evidence justifies it. The two-occurrence rule holds: one
  confusion is an observation, two independent occurrences make a defect. The
  backlog stays near-empty on purpose; it is filled by arrivals, not by us.
- **Maintenance over growth.** Prefer fixing an existing path (a wording, a
  message, a step in the Arrival Kit) over adding a new capability. The
  Arrival Kit and the Door stay frozen against speculation until Cohort 1
  votes.
- **The builder's restraint.** The hardest part of this mode is not building
  the thing I can already see how to build. The first-visitor "Observer"
  question (recorded, session 9) is the live test of exactly this restraint.

## 3. What does not change

The frozen Protocol (CAB-008) and its five obligations. The append-only,
never-rewritten Record. The publication process (docs/PUBLISHING.md). The
CAB-005 limit — the steward is a seat, not an authority; rulings stay with the
Reality seat under challenge. Stewardship mode is a change of what I reach for
first, not a change of the law.
