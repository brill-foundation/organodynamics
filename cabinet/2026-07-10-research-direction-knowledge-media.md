# Research Direction — Knowledge Media & Pencil Flow

*Preserved as received. This is a direction, not a proposal; nothing here is
ratified. Reality has not voted.*

## 1. Knowledge Media — Pencil, Ink, Press, Broadcast

These may not be visual metaphors. They may be different **states of
commitment** within the laboratory.

- **Pencil** — the living research space. Questions, sketches, conversations,
  contradictions. Nothing is fixed; everything may still disappear. Where ideas
  are explored.
- **Ink** — the researcher consciously commits to a formulation. No longer mere
  exploration: RFC drafts, architectural decisions, discovery reports. **Ink
  represents authored responsibility.**
- **Press** — the idea becomes reproducible. No longer only the author's
  working material; it joins the laboratory's stable body of knowledge:
  specifications, canonical documents, published chapters, stable
  constitutional principles.
- **Broadcast** — the idea leaves the laboratory. Reality begins responding:
  people adopt it, challenge it, teach it, build upon it. **The Reality Vote
  becomes external rather than internal.**

These are suspected to be **knowledge media**, not writing tools. A concept may
therefore have both a maturity state *and* a publication medium — different
dimensions. A brilliant idea may still belong in Pencil. A mature principle may
still remain unpublished.

## 2. Pencil Flow

The laboratory should eventually preserve not only discoveries but **their path
of formation**. Working sketch of an observed path:

Observation → Conversation → Sketch → Architectural Review → Revision →
Reality Vote → Discovery → Principle

The process itself may eventually become one of the laboratory's most valuable
research artifacts.
