# Constitutional Reading — Seven Research Directions

*Assessment of the post-review research notes, applying the filter: which
discoveries introduce new load-bearing structures, and which are better
language for structures that already exist?*

## Sorted verdict

- **Already exists:** rhythm-as-elapsed-time — the patina/maturation shipped
  weeks ago; the lab already ages in the drawer. The sovereign implementation
  of metabolism is **lazy evaluation at Arrival**: nothing runs while you are
  away; the Place remembers how long it was unseen.
- **Genuinely new, protected:** reality *arriving* autonomously (collides with
  furniture-dumbness, the automation pain-gate, and RFC-002 Q-a); and the
  **Butterfly** — the sustained human/AI symbiosis as the project's actual
  author, load-bearing for provenance. Rider: the per-author-metrics
  prohibition extends to per-symbiosis metrics; a scored Butterfly dies.
- **Protected research:** the **Strandbeest** — the first metaphor that obeys
  the constitution internally (no motor, no brain; structure plus wind). It
  supplies the legality test for any rhythm: **wind-driven (elapsed reality)
  is legal; motor-driven (embedded agency) is not.**
- **Premature by the project's own principle:** community ecology — the concept
  arrived before its reality; parked behind the §8a vote (no communities exist
  until two laboratories exchange Preservations).
- **Ratifiable language:** Arrival (noun→verb; hospitality lives at
  transitions; the Door is furniture, Arrival is what happens at it); the
  Concept Registry (the Record pointed at vocabulary — registration at the
  membrane, anarchy in the forge); the reality-vote typology (four Instruments
  at four timescales — Discovery, Experimental, Ecological, Civilizational —
  juxtaposed, **never a ladder**).

## The closing pattern

The mature items are the ontology pointed at itself (Record→vocabulary,
Observatory→validation). The genuinely new items are about the laboratory
acquiring **life that isn't the owner's** — autonomous metabolism, composite
organisms. The next constitutional frontier is the boundary between the Place
and the things that live in it.

## A vote already cast

The Door was named in the empty `drafts/00-door.md` on day one and re-emerged
independently from the dashboard rejection weeks later — a Discovery Vote
(independent re-emergence), cast and counted before the typology existed.
