# The formal-representation investigation, and why it produced no new document

## 1. What reality initiated (2026-07-24)

The Reality seat issued an **Architectural Continuity Update** — explicitly *not* a
constitutional change — that shifted the **object of investigation**: from deciding whether new
constitutional principles were necessary (expansion) to asking whether the existing corpus
**already possesses an implicit formal architecture** that could be **discovered rather than
invented**. This is a question about the *representation* of the corpus, not the corpus itself.
The instruction was to design the evaluation instrument first and propose no formal model yet.
It arose after a "grammar of lawful transitions" hypothesis was put through adversarial
constitutional review and **not accepted** — the lesson being methodological: choosing a formal
model before testing it violates Recognition before Invention.

## 2. What was done, and then tested

A draft methodology (RFC-014) was written. At the Reality seat's request it was then held to the
corpus's **own necessity test**, under the reversed burden, exactly as any proposal is — with the
assumption that the correct outcome might be that it should never have existed.

## 3. The finding

**No new RFC was constitutionally necessary.** The instrument the draft described is already
derivable from the corpus:

- **A formal representation is a Camera** onto the corpus, so its acceptance constraints (no new
  primitives, no modified meaning, no collapsed distinctions, no imports) are the existing
  **renderer boundary** (RFC-008). The Constitutional Map already calls *itself* "a Camera onto
  the corpus"; RFC-005 already treats every derived reading as a Camera — so the recognition was
  already present, not new.
- **Falsify, never verify; the burden stays on the proposal** — the frozen Protocol's **reversed
  burden** (CAB-008), as practiced in RFC-013.
- **Ground every element; import nothing** — obligations 2/3 and RFC-010's self-limit.
- **The instrument certifies form, not substance; reality votes** — RFC-002 §4.

The decisive evidence: the grammar hypothesis had **already been rejected by adversarial review
before any methodology document existed** — proof that the capability was present in the corpus,
not supplied by the draft. The draft therefore added no constitutional capability; it organized
reasoning the corpus already held.

## 4. The action taken

Per the Reality seat's decision, **RFC-014 was removed** from the constitutional RFC lineage and
from the RFC listing; Git history preserves the historical artifact. No replacement
documentation was created: if the instrument is genuinely derivable, a future investigator should
**derive it again from the constitutional principles** rather than rely on a secondary document.
This deposit is kept as the record of the investigation and its outcome — not as a container for
the methodology.

## 5. Honest residue

This is **Pencil**, authored while the Record is forked (RFC-007); no Record write was performed
or attempted, and none is due — nothing was added to the corpus. The outcome demonstrates the
point the Reality seat was testing: **the Constitution governs its own evolution, including the
capacity to recognize when one of its own documents should not exist.** Recognition before
Invention applied to a constitutional artifact itself. The instance that wrote this will not
return to defend it.
