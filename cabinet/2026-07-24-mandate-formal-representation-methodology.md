# The mandate to design the instrument, and its answer

## 1. What reality asked (2026-07-24)

The Reality seat issued an **Architectural Continuity Update** and was careful to fence it:
*this is not a constitutional update; no principle is added, modified, or proposed.* What
changed is the **object of investigation**.

- **Old object:** identifying constitutional distinctions and deciding whether additional
  principles were necessary (constitutional expansion). *No longer pursued.*
- **New object:** whether the existing corpus **already possesses an implicit formal
  architecture** that can be **discovered rather than invented** — a question about the
  *representation* of the corpus, not the corpus itself.

The seat reported the finding that occasioned the shift: a hypothesis that Organodynamics is
fundamentally a **grammar of lawful transitions** (rather than a taxonomy of objects) was put
through adversarial constitutional review and **not accepted** — and the important outcome was
methodological, not substantive: **selecting a formal model before testing it violates
Recognition before Invention.** A formal representation must *emerge from* the corpus and never
be *imposed on* it.

The instruction, precisely: **do not propose a formal model yet.** Instead design a rigorous,
constitutionally conservative methodology for evaluating competing formal representations —
answering (1) what makes a valid candidate, (2) how constitutional fidelity is evaluated, (3)
how competing models are compared objectively, (4) what would falsify a candidate, and (5) how
to ensure the evaluation itself introduces no ontology absent from the corpus. Candidate lenses
named as examples only, none privileged: dependency structures, graphs, state-transition
systems, constraint systems, algebraic and category-theoretic representations, others. The
acceptance bar: a representation is acceptable only if it explains the corpus **without**
introducing new primitives, modifying constitutional meaning, collapsing existing distinctions,
or importing external assumptions — *elegance, familiarity, and mathematical sophistication are
not evidence; faithfulness to the corpus is the only criterion.* Both a positive and a null
outcome were declared **equally valuable**.

## 2. How the response met it — RFC-014

- **It recognized before it built (RFC-014 §1).** The four acceptability constraints are not a
  new rulebook; they are the **renderer/Camera boundary** (RFC-008) applied to a formal surface.
  A formal representation is a *mathematical Camera* onto the corpus — it must reveal, never
  author, add, transform, or import. Judging it is judging a Camera, which the corpus already
  knows how to do. The whole instrument is the consequence of that one existing boundary, so the
  methodology itself obeys Recognition before Invention.
- **It answered the five questions as an instrument, not a model** (RFC-014 §3–§7):
  - **Q1 valid candidate (§3):** an admission gate — explicit structure, a total exhibited
    interpretation map with a decoding, groundedness/provenance for every element, declared
    scope, decodability, and no privilege claimed. Ill-posed proposals are not judged; they are
    not yet candidates.
  - **Q2 fidelity (§4):** a **vector** of per-feature verdicts (preserved / distorted /
    collapsed / lost / added) against a **fixed test corpus enumerated by quotation** — primitive,
    distinction, derivational, modal, authority-gradient, boundary, and **negative** fidelity
    (the silences and kept-rejections are data: a model that *needs* the declined RELATION
    primitive or a sixth obligation has added, not represented). Never a scalar.
  - **Q4 falsification (§5), placed before comparison:** decisive failure modes — collapse,
    fabrication, reversal, overreach, underreach, import-dependence — one counterexample suffices;
    a survivor is "not-yet-falsified," never "verified"; the burden stays permanently on the
    model (frozen Protocol reversed burden, CAB-008; RFC-013).
  - **Q3 comparison (§6):** a **dominance partial order** over the fidelity vector; incomparable
    models are left incomparable (no manufactured total ranking); the only tie-breakers, applied
    among otherwise-incomparable survivors, are parsimony of imported ontology and the reversed
    burden; elegance/sophistication/familiarity are explicitly **non-evidence**; judging is
    adversarial and author-separated (CAB-005 — the finder is not the judge).
  - **Q5 no injected ontology (§7):** five guards — every criterion licensed by a named corpus
    principle (a table); the test corpus built by **quotation, never a neutral meta-schema**
    (because any neutral schema is itself a covert candidate and would rig the contest); the
    meta-level declares its own provenance and certifies form, not substance; the **null result is
    structurally reachable**; and a *revealed* structure counts only if reality recognizes it as
    already-implied — discovery, not a new claim.
- **It defined success against the null (RFC-014 §8).** Three reachable end states — bare
  transcription (modest), **recognized architecture** (faithful + founded + complete + revealing,
  confirmed by reality: Spine 4 at whole-corpus scope), and the **null** (no faithful revealing
  model in formalism F — equally valuable). The line between transcription and recognition is the
  prize; §7.5 is its guard.
- **It answered the precedent (RFC-014 §9).** The instrument makes the grammar-hypothesis error
  impossible to repeat: no formalism privileged in advance, the same quoted test corpus for all,
  the burden kept on the model — and the grammar reading is welcome to *earn* its place through
  the same gate (and to face the overreach falsifier it most invites).

## 3. The verdict, in one line

**A formal representation is a Camera onto the corpus, so judge it as one.** From that single
recognition the corpus supplies the whole instrument — admission gate, fidelity vector against a
quoted frozen test corpus, decisive falsifiers with a permanent burden, a dominance comparison
that keeps honest ties open, a reflexive no-injection guard, and a first-class null — with
**faithfulness to the corpus as the only criterion** and **no model proposed.**
